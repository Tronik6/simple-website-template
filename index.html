Omega Space

Omega Space is a fast-paced space shooter where you play as a lone ship fleeing your planetary sector after a mysterious mass extinction devastates your homeworld. As you escape across the stars, you’ll face relentless alien swarms and investigate the source of the catastrophe. But you're not alone in the void: lurking in the shadows of deep space is Myxar, Lord of the Acid Veil—a grotesque, tentacled entity born of sour matter and dripping with toxic purpose. Half legend, half nightmare, Myxar waits to smother your mission in a storm of corrosive wrath.

Will you escape the veil, uncover the truth, and survive the bitter end?

Prepare for a zesty, high-stakes odyssey in Omega Space, where the juice... fights back.
